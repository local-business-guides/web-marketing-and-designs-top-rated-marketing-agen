Services, timing, costs, and common terms

A marketing engagement may include planning, content creation, website improvements, campaign setup, and ongoing review. Ask which parts are included and how the work will be coordinated.

Key facts to discuss:
- What it covers: SEO, content, social media, video, branding, website optimization, targeted advertising, or a combination.
- Typical timing: Timing depends on the work. A focused task and a broader, ongoing campaign require different schedules. Ask for milestones and what your team needs to provide.
- What affects cost: The number of services, amount of content or creative work, campaign scope, and how much ongoing support you need can all affect the overall cost. Request a scope that spells out those elements.

When you compare providers, look beyond a list of services. Ask for a plain explanation of the plan, who is responsible for each task, how often you'll review the work, and what information will be used to assess results. Be cautious of proposals that don't connect the work to your stated goals.

A few terms you'll hear:
- SEO: Work intended to improve a website's visibility in search results.
- Lead generation: Marketing activities aimed at bringing potential customers to a business.
- Website optimization: Changes intended to make a website more useful for its goals and visitors.
