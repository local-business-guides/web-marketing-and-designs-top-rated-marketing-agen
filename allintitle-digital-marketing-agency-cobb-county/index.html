Scope, schedule, cost, and terms to compare

Before committing, ask for a written explanation of the work and how the agency expects to carry it out. A clear scope helps you understand what’s included and makes it easier to compare proposals.

  • Coverage: Services may include SEO, content, social media, video, branding, website optimization, targeted advertising, and lead generation. Ask which items are recommended for your particular goals.
  • Timing: The schedule varies with the campaign’s scope, the services involved, and the time needed for reviews or materials. Ask about the planned stages and when you’ll be asked to provide input.
  • Cost factors: General factors include the number of services, the amount of content or creative work, campaign requirements, and whether work is ongoing. Ask what’s included and what could change the scope.
  • Provider comparison: Compare the reasoning behind each plan, the specific work promised, how communication will happen, and how results will be discussed. A long service list alone doesn’t tell you whether the plan fits your needs.

Some terms you may hear: SEO means improving a site’s visibility in search results. Targeted advertising means directing ads toward a selected audience. Lead generation refers to attracting potential customers and encouraging them to take a next step. Ask for examples tied to the work being proposed.
