A campaign can include several kinds of work. SEO focuses on improving how people find relevant pages through search. Content marketing uses useful material to support your message. Social media marketing and video marketing help present that message in different formats. Website optimization looks at how your site serves visitors, while targeted advertising places paid promotion around a chosen audience or objective. Lead generation is the work of encouraging and capturing inquiries.

- Coverage: Services may include SEO, content, social media, video, branding, website optimization, advertising, and lead generation.
- Timing: The schedule depends on the work involved, the materials available, and how many services are part of the plan. Ask for the proposed steps and check-in points before work begins.
