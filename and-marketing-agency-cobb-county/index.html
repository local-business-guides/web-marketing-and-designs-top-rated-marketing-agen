Scope, schedule, cost, and terms to understand

A project may begin with a conversation about your audience and goals, then move into planning and delivery of the agreed services. The exact workflow depends on the scope, so ask what happens first, what decisions you’ll need to make, and how ongoing work is reviewed.

Key facts for your provider conversation:
- Coverage: The plan could involve SEO, written content, social media, video, branding, website work, targeted ads, or lead generation.
- Timing: A schedule varies with the number and type of tasks, review steps, and whether support continues over time. Ask for a timeline based on the actual scope, not a general promise.
- Cost factors: Services selected, creative needs, campaign breadth, and ongoing involvement can affect the cost. Ask providers to describe these parts clearly so you can compare like with like.

A few terms in everyday language:
- Content marketing: Creating and sharing content as part of a marketing plan.
- Targeted advertising: Ads planned for a chosen audience.
- Online branding: How your business is presented and recognized across digital channels.

These definitions are starting points. Ask each provider how the terms apply to the specific work in its proposal. A useful comparison includes deliverables, timing, your responsibilities, and how updates will be handled.
